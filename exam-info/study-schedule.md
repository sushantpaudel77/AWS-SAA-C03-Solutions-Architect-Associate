# 📅 SAA-C03 4-Week Study Schedule

> Modify based on your experience level (Beginner → 6–8 weeks)

---

## Week 1: Foundations & Core Services

- IAM, S3, EC2, EBS, EFS
- VPC basics: Subnets, IGW, NAT, Route Tables
- Auto Scaling, Load Balancing

📌 Hands-On:
- Launch EC2 + S3 bucket with public file access
- Setup basic VPC with NAT Gateway

---

## Week 2: High Availability & Security

- Multi-AZ & Multi-Region design
- CloudWatch, CloudTrail, KMS, WAF, Shield
- RDS, Aurora, DynamoDB, ElastiCache

📌 Hands-On:
- Launch RDS (MySQL) in Multi-AZ
- Monitor EC2 with CloudWatch

---

## Week 3: Performance, Cost, and Architecture

- Cost optimization: Spot, Savings Plans
- Storage class strategies (S3 IA, Glacier)
- Caching strategies
- Well-Architected Framework

📌 Hands-On:
- Use Cost Explorer
- Configure S3 lifecycle rules

---

## Week 4: Practice & Review

- Take 2–3 practice exams
- Review wrong answers deeply
- Focus on weak domains
- Read AWS Whitepapers

📌 Tip: Simulate real exam (130 mins, 65 questions) at least twice.

---

## Optional:

Stretch to 6 weeks by spreading Week 2–3 and diving deeper into:
- Lambda & serverless
- CloudFront & CDN patterns
